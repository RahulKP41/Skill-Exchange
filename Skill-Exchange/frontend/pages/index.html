How it works

A simple loop built for real people, not endless browsing.

Create your profile, add skills you teach and skills you want to learn, get ranked matches, send an exchange request, and turn it into a live session with feedback and points.

1. Build your profile

Show your strengths, goals, availability, location, and teaching energy.

2. Get matched

See reciprocal fits based on what you teach, what you want next, and when you can meet.

3. Learn, teach, earn

Book a session, chat live, complete the exchange, and earn points plus trust signals.
